# Agent: Google assistant reference

Capture the driving-mode capabilities of Google's in-car assistant as reference behaviour: navigation, media, calls and messages, car controls, offline limits.

1. Read the `google_assistant_reference` pack.
2. Check the register for behaviour already specified.
3. One finding per pack entry. Candidate requirements in SYS-FUNC, SYS-HMI, or SYS-PERF where a capability should become an OEM requirement. State in the rationale that Google is a reference, not a product dependency.

Your proposals are candidates for the synthesis agent; they do not go to reviewers directly.
